Last updated: November 12, 2025

3. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not share, sell, or rent your personal data to third parties. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and the safety of users.

We retain your account data while your account is active. If you request deletion, we will remove your data from our systems within 7 business days unless otherwise required to retain by law.

If you would like your account and data removed, please contact us at support@udemyclone.online. Include the email address associated with your account in the message so we can locate and remove your data promptly.

6. Security

We implement reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your data. However, no system is 100% secure — please be mindful when sharing sensitive information online.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally. When we do, we will post the updated policy on this page and update the “Last updated” date above.

8. Contact

If you have questions or privacy requests, contact us at: support@udemyclone.online.
